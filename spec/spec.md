An offline-ready laundry POS and operations management system with role-based access for cashiers and store owners. Cashiers can register customers, create orders combining weight-based (kg) and item-based (pcs) services with rush/express pricing options, update laundry status across workflow stages, apply prepaid customer deposits, and print receipt summaries. Store owners can configure service catalogs and pricing, monitor consumable inventory levels with low-stock warnings, log daily shop expenses, and review financial and order summary dashboards.
