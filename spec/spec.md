An offline-first laundry management and point-of-sale (POS) system with role-based access control for Store Owners and Cashiers. Cashiers can manage customer profiles (with order histories and quick WhatsApp/call actions), create multi-item laundry orders (supporting kg and pcs units, custom invoice prefixes, estimated completion dates, and flexible payment statuses: Lunas, Belum Lunas, Sebagian via Cash or Transfer), track order workflow status (Pending → Proses → Selesai → Diambil), print thermal receipts (58mm/80mm), and share digital receipts via WhatsApp. Store Owners have full administrative access: managing service packages (pricing, turnaround duration, active/inactive toggle), viewing real-time dashboards (today's revenue, status breakdowns, weekly revenue charts, recent orders), analyzing sales reports with interactive date filters and Excel (.xlsx) export, managing user accounts, and configuring laundry shop info, invoice numbering, and printer settings.
